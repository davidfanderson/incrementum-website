UNDERSTAND WHAT YOU SHARE

Your route stays on your phone.

Raw GPS coordinates and route shapes aren’t uploaded to Incrementum’s backend. Saving sends derived map-cell identifiers and private activity summaries instead.

Those cells can reveal fine-grained areas you visited, and resulting territory is visible to signed-in players. They are location information.
