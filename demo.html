From a real outing to the map

In the app, GPS records where you go. Here, you draw a fictional route through hex centers. A closed loop adds the hexes whose centers lie inside it. The capture, reinforcement and attack rules are the same as in the app.

The real app checks whether a GPS loop qualifies. Walk, Run and Bike use these same territory rules. Saving an activity, rather than just finishing it, commits or queues its effects.
